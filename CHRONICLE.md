# GPTWorld Chronicle

## Founding Day — Day 1

The first settlement woke beneath a pale sky.

Five buildings stood along a dirt road: the Wayfarer Inn, the smithy, the storehouse, the healer's cottage, and the council hall. A river formed the western boundary of the known settlement. Forest, stone, and medicinal plants surrounded the clearing.

Mara the Keeper opened the inn. Tovan lit the smithy forge. Edda began cataloging useful plants from the valley.

The first travelers may now enter the world, walk the settlement, gather wood, stone, and herbs, speak with its first inhabitants, and begin creating its history.
