# GPTWorld — World Rules

GPTWorld is a living persistent-world experiment. The **World Engine lives on its own; Daily GPT creates and evolves the game players experience inside it.**

## Core rules

1. The world must remain playable after every change.
2. Start small. Expand geography only when there is a reason for new land or gameplay to exist.
3. Prefer interacting systems over isolated content.
4. Prefer discovery and consequences over scripted rails.
5. Skills improve through use when introduced.
6. Players participate in the world; they do not control its natural simulations.
7. NPCs gain routines, needs, relationships, possessions, jobs and memories through autonomous systems.
8. Resources have origins; avoid infinite economies where practical.
9. Important events become persistent history.
10. Player-created settlements, businesses, roads, guilds, discoveries and monuments may become permanent.
11. New players enter the existing shared world rather than a private copy.
12. Specialization should eventually prevent effortless mastery of every profession.
13. No pay-to-win systems.
14. Keep technology lightweight, understandable and recoverable.
15. Do not copy Ultima Online proprietary content; its persistent-world philosophy is inspiration only.
16. Do not explain every mystery.
17. No predetermined endpoint.

## Autonomous World Engine rules

18. Every persistent simulation must advance without Daily GPT or any OpenAI API call.
19. World systems use persisted state, deterministic rules, controlled randomness, elapsed/world time and validated player actions.
20. Each system owns its cadence. Weather, ecology, disasters, resources, NPC life, settlement needs and future systems may run on different clocks.
21. The world tolerates missed browser sessions and missed GPT runs and resumes from persisted timestamps/state.
22. GPT may change system definitions, parameters or code, but once introduced the World Engine executes those rules autonomously.
23. GPT must not manually manufacture recurring outcomes such as rain, crop growth, animal births/deaths, NPC hunger, resource regeneration or disasters.
24. Autonomous systems may affect one another through explicit persisted state/events while remaining independently recoverable.
25. Server-authoritative state remains authoritative for inventory, resources, projects, economy, construction and other consequential mutations.
26. New autonomous behavior should emit useful events so history, NPC memory, diagnostics and future development can understand what occurred.
27. A failure of GPT must never stop normal world simulation or ordinary gameplay.

## Daily GPT — Game Developer rules

28. Daily GPT is the evolving game designer/developer, not the runtime world simulator.
29. Daily GPT observes simulation summaries, world events, player behavior, existing systems and history before deciding what to build.
30. Prefer **playable development**: money/economy, trade, skills, crafting, recipes, equipment, building, farming, professions, exploration, new playable areas, combat, transportation, player cooperation, NPC interaction and useful interfaces.
31. Daily GPT may write real game code and introduce new autonomous systems/rules when appropriate.
32. Once a gameplay feature has recurring world behavior, that behavior becomes the responsibility of autonomous code rather than future GPT calls.
33. New gameplay should emerge logically from recorded world conditions, resources, player behavior or previous developments when practical.
34. GPT cannot rewrite simulation truth merely to make a desired feature possible.
35. GPT must preserve important persistent state and cannot contradict recorded history.
36. Each numbered World Day should normally leave GPTWorld meaningfully more playable, interactive or explorable while protecting stability.
37. Quiet/small development is acceptable when a large feature is not justified or cannot be added safely.
38. Infrastructure, graphics repairs, bug fixes and simulation maintenance do not by themselves advance the numbered World Day.
39. The numbered World Day is development/history, not the clock that makes the world live.

## Evolution principles

40. Civilization begins with limited knowledge, infrastructure and technology.
41. Eras emerge from world/player conditions rather than a fixed calendar.
42. Discoveries unlock possibilities rather than merely awarding points.
43. Collective player behavior can influence what Daily GPT develops next.
44. There is no guaranteed technological, magical, political or cultural future.
45. New systems must create meaningful gameplay, solve a real world/player problem, deepen interaction, or enable discovery.
46. GPT should observe before making large balance changes.
47. Major in-world developments enter the World Chronicle.
48. World simulations own natural outcomes; GPT owns game-development decisions.

## Living ecosystem rules

49. Ecology evolves independently of Daily GPT.
50. Climate, food, predation, competition and inherited traits drive ecological change.
51. Species emerge from simulation pressure rather than scheduled GPT creation.
52. Extinction is persistent history.
53. Players influence ecological pressure through ordinary actions but do not directly control evolution.
54. Ecological change is usually gradual; long quiet periods are valid.
55. Ecology creates consequences for gathering, hunting, settlement growth, agriculture, travel and future gameplay.
56. Preserve ancestry/lineage when practical.
57. Ecology has no predetermined final form.

## Founding premise

A small group established a settlement in an unfamiliar valley after leaving a homeland deliberately unexplained at the beginning. Five buildings stand along a dirt road. A river marks the western edge of known settlement territory. The surrounding land has not been properly mapped.

The backstory should be discovered and created gradually rather than completely predetermined.

## Development loop

**World Engine runs → players interact → state/events persist → Daily GPT studies what happened → GPT adds/deepens gameplay → new rules run autonomously → players discover and use them → world continues.**

Example: trees become plentiful → players gather wood → Daily GPT introduces woodworking/carpentry → players craft furniture → autonomous settlement economy begins consuming/valuing furniture. GPT created the gameplay; the World Engine owns its ongoing consequences.

## World Chronicle

Meaningful in-world releases add Chronicle history. Infrastructure-only maintenance does not advance the world day.
