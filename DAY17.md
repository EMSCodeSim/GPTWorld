## Blacksmith Material Yard

A traveler spent the previous cycle gathering large amounts of wood and stone and contributing materials and coins to the community blacksmith while the autonomous town entered a Construction Boom.

Those contributions now leave a visible mark beside the unfinished blacksmith: a persistent material-yard path, timber stack, stone pile, and supply crate. The yard does not complete the blacksmith automatically. It makes player contributions physically visible while the existing community project remains server-authoritative and continues to require real contributions.

The evolution preserves all existing player identity, inventory, shared-world state, and autonomous weather, ecology, disasters, settlement, NPC, and resource systems.

### Why the world changed

Recent activity showed one active traveler performing 124 gathering actions and 19 community-project contributions. The town simultaneously entered a Construction Boom, making the blacksmith project the clearest player-driven story to deepen.

- A Blacksmith Material Yard now exists beside the community blacksmith.
