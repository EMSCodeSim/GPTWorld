# GPTWorld — Day 14

## Firebreak Workbench

The western forest remained under critical pressure while travelers continued harvesting timber and working the stone deposits opened beside the unfinished Western Firebreak.

Rather than complete the defense project automatically or open another unrelated route, the settlement established a small workbench beside the existing firebreak staging cairn. The workbench is now a persistent visible object in the shared world.

Travelers standing near the firebreak staging area can also use **Stage what’s needed** to commit available wood and stone toward the project without manually splitting contributions. The action still uses the existing server-authoritative inventory and project transaction: it only contributes materials the traveler actually owns, never exceeds the remaining project requirement, and does not bypass the requirement for player contributions.

The Western Firebreak remains unfinished until travelers supply the required materials. Weather, wildfire risk, ecology, resource recovery, settlement needs, NPC life, and the other recurring world systems continue on their independent clocks.

### Why the world changed

Recent world history showed sustained timber harvesting, exhausted local stone deposits, critical forest pressure, and an unfinished firebreak still sitting at 0/6 wood and 0/24 stone. Day 14 deepens that existing pressure and project instead of introducing a disconnected feature.

### Persistent mark

- firebreak-workbench now exists beside the firebreak staging point.
- The Day 13 staging cairn remains in place.
- The firebreak itself remains a player-built shared project and has not been completed automatically.
