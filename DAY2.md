# Day 2 — The Western Crossing

The first settlement has a reason to look beyond its boundary.

The council has chosen the settlement's first communal project: a crossing over the western river. The project establishes the first world-driven development goal rather than simply adding more scenery.

## World state

- The western river remains impassable.
- The proposed crossing requires timber and stone gathered from the surrounding land.
- Completion of the crossing will open the first new explorable territory west of the settlement.
- What is found beyond the river is intentionally not predetermined here; it should be introduced when the crossing becomes part of the playable world.

## Development rule introduced

Future geographic expansion should be earned through world events, discoveries, or player activity rather than appearing arbitrarily. The western crossing is the first example of that rule.
