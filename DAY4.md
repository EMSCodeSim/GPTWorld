# Day 4 — The Western Survey

Travel beyond the completed Western Crossing and a well-supplied communal storehouse encouraged the settlement to cautiously map the western bank.

## Playable Day 4 evolution

- A short persistent survey trail now extends west from the completed crossing.
- A visible survey marker records the first deliberate mapping beyond the river.
- Three newly mapped resource nodes — wood, stone, and herbs — now exist west of the crossing.
- These nodes are registered through the persistent rendering contract and use the existing server-authoritative gathering system.
- Their depletion and regrowth continue on independent clocks and do not depend on future numbered world-day runs.
- Existing player identity, position, inventory, storehouse stockpile, Western Crossing, NPC state, weather, settlement needs, ecology, and world history remain unchanged.

## Why it matters

Recent travelers have continued gathering and depositing resources after finishing the Western Crossing, while the settlement remains well supplied. Rather than inventing a large arbitrary expansion, Day 4 gives that behavior a modest consequence: the first mapped route and gatherable foothold on the western side.

The trail and marker are persistent visible marks in the playable world, so the crossing now leads somewhere that reflects the settlement's actual history.
