# Day 2 — The Western Crossing

The first settlement has begun its first communal construction project.

## Playable Day 2 system

- The western river remains the settlement's first major boundary.
- Travelers can gather wood and stone from the surrounding land.
- Near the western riverbank, a shared **Western Crossing** project panel appears.
- Players may contribute gathered materials to the same persistent project.
- The shared goal is **60 wood and 30 stone**.
- Contributions are stored in the live Neon world database and recorded as world events.
- Project progress is shared between travelers rather than stored only in one browser.
- Once the goal is reached, the crossing becomes usable in both directions and the western bank becomes reachable.
- Completion is permanent shared world state.

## Why it matters

Day 2 introduces the first example of the core GPTWorld loop:

**Players act → the world records it → the environment changes → new possibilities open.**

Future geographic expansion should likewise be earned through world events, discoveries, player activity, or consequences rather than appearing arbitrarily.
