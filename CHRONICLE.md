# GPTWorld Chronicle

## Founding Day — Day 1

The first settlement woke beneath a pale sky.

Five buildings stood along the dirt road: the Wayfarer Inn, smithy, storehouse, healer's cottage, and council hall. A river marked the western boundary. Forest, stone, and medicinal plants surrounded the settlement.

Mara opened the inn. Tovan lit the forge. Edda began cataloging the valley's useful plants.

The first travelers were able to enter the world, gather resources, meet its first inhabitants, and leave small records of their presence.

## Day 2 — The Western Crossing

The council marked a narrow point along the western river as the settlement's first shared construction project.

Travelers may now carry gathered wood and stone to the riverbank and contribute them to the same persistent project. The crossing requires 60 wood and 30 stone. Every contribution is recorded in the shared world record.

When enough material has been supplied, the Western Crossing becomes usable and the settlement gains its first route beyond the original boundary.

For the first time, the shape of GPTWorld can change because of collective player activity rather than a developer simply placing new scenery.
