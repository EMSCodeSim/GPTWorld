# Day 7 — The Timber Line

The settlement has chosen to keep expanding even as repeated cutting pushes the nearby forest into critical pressure.

## Playable Day 7 evolution

- A narrow logging spur now branches from the western survey route and remains visible through the persistent rendering system.
- Two newly marked timber stands can be gathered through the existing server-authoritative resource system.
- The new stands carry more wood than an ordinary pine, but regrow much more slowly, making the cost of continued expansion visible in play rather than hiding it in a counter.
- A persistent timber marker records where the settlement deliberately pushed farther into pressured forest.
- Existing player identity, position, inventory, projects, crafting, weather, ecology, NPC routines, resource lifecycles, and private worlds remain unchanged.

## Why it happened

Forest pressure reached critical levels after sustained harvesting, including complete cutting of multiple trees. At the current forest milestone, a traveler explicitly chose **Continue expansion**, accepting greater habitat pressure. Day 7 turns that choice into a physical and playable consequence: more timber is available, but it lies along a visible new scar in the landscape and returns slowly after cutting.

The forest simulation and resource clocks remain autonomous. Day 7 does not manually regrow trees or advance ecology; it adds a new place where future player choices can create further consequences.
