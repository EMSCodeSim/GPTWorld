# Day 9 — Edda’s Herb Plot

Travelers spent the previous day repeatedly gathering medicinal herbs and returning to speak with Edda. Rather than expanding the settlement again, Day 9 makes that existing relationship visible in the world.

## Playable Day 9 evolution

- Edda’s long-mentioned herb plots near the healer’s cottage are now physically represented in the shared playable world.
- A small cultivated bed, drying rack, and server-authoritative medicinal herb patch appear near her daytime work area.
- The cultivated patch uses the existing persistent resource system, so gathering is distance-validated, inventory updates remain atomic, depletion is shared, and regrowth continues independently of numbered world days.
- The patch is deliberately modest: it supplements wild gathering rather than replacing exploration.
- No autonomous weather, disaster, ecology, NPC, settlement, or resource clocks are manually ticked by the day transition.

## Why this evolution

Recent activity was concentrated on medicinal-herb gathering and repeated interactions with Edda the Healer. The world therefore deepens an existing place and NPC routine instead of opening unrelated territory. Edda’s simulation already describes daytime work at herb plots; Day 9 closes the gap between that persistent routine and what players can actually see and use.
