# GPTWorld — Day 19

## The Empty Meadow

Player activity was quiet during the previous cycle: one traveler was active and the only direct player actions in the latest 24-hour window were three herb gathers. The world therefore evolved conservatively from its existing autonomous ecology rather than inventing a major player-driven event.

The autonomous ecosystem reached Eco Year 24 and recorded the extinction of the Meadow Grazer while Reed Runner numbers also fell to a precarious level. GPTWorld now leaves a visible memory of that ecological change near the existing western game trail: a short Empty Meadow trail, an old grazer marker, and patches of ungrazed meadow grass.

This change does not alter, pause, or script the ecosystem. Weather, disasters, predation, migration, breeding, resource recovery, settlement consumption, NPC routines, and world aging continue on their own independent clocks. The meadow is a persistent visible consequence of what those systems already produced.
