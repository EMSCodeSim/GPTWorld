# Day 9 — Edda’s Herb Garden

Travelers have continued harvesting medicinal herbs, and recent world activity shows repeated visits to Edda the Healer. Day 9 turns that existing behavior into a small persistent gathering place beside the healer’s cottage rather than opening unrelated new territory.

## Playable Day 9 evolution

- A marked herb garden now appears beside the healer’s cottage.
- A short visible path and garden marker make the location easy to recognize in the shared world.
- Two new herb patches are registered through the existing persistent rendering contract.
- The herb patches use the existing server-authoritative resource system, so gathering updates persistent inventory rather than browser-local state.
- Each patch has its own 45-minute regrowth clock and continues to recover independently of the numbered World Day.
- No weather, ecology, NPC, settlement, or other autonomous system is manually advanced by Day 9.

## Why this evolution

Recent player activity was concentrated around resource gathering, including repeated medicinal-herb harvesting, and Edda remained one of the NPCs players interacted with. The settlement is also already well supplied, so the next development is intentionally small: deepen the healer/herbalism loop players are already using instead of adding another large expansion.

The garden is a persistent physical addition to the playable environment and remains part of the world on future days unless a later in-world event changes it.
